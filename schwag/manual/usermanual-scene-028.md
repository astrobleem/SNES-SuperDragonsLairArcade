# SCENE 28: THE DRAGON'S LAIR

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-028.svg)

![Dragon's Lair](images/cc_fixed_shot.png)

**The Final Battle**

This is it. The deepest chamber of the castle. Mountains of gold coins fill the cavern, glittering in the light of Singe's smoldering breath. The dragon himself lies coiled atop his hoard -- a massive, warty beast of green scales, purple horns, and terrible claws. And there, floating in a shimmering crystal sphere at the far end of the chamber, is Princess Daphne.

She sees you. She calls out. And that cry wakes the dragon.

Singe's yellow eyes snap open. Fire erupts from his jaws. His spiked tail sweeps across the chamber, scattering gold and shattering stone pillars. The final battle has begun -- and there is no retreat. Somewhere in the dragon's hoard lies the enchanted sword Dragonfang, the only weapon that can pierce his hide. And around his neck hangs the golden key, the only thing that can shatter Daphne's prison.

*   **Threat:** Singe's devastating fire breath, crushing tail, and razor claws. The dragon is fast, furious, and fights to kill. This is the most dangerous room in the game.
*   **Action:** Use the stone pillars for cover against his fire! Search the gold for the enchanted sword Dragonfang. Find it, strike true, and claim the key from around his neck to free the Princess!
*   **Tip:** Singe sleeps lightly -- move carefully at first. When he wakes, use the pillars to block his fire breath. The sword is buried in the gold near a large diamond. One perfect throw is all it takes. Make it count. The fate of the kingdom depends on this moment.
