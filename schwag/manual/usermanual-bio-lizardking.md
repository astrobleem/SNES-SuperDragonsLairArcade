# THE LIZARD KING

**Ruler of the Pot of Gold**

*   **Species:** Humanoid Reptile
*   **Skin:** Green scales, large bumpy warts, yellow eyes
*   **Attire:** Golden crown, purple robes, golden belt
*   **Weapon:** Golden scepter (doubles as a mace)
*   **Domain:** The Treasury
*   **Personality:** Greedy, cunning, relentless

---

### Lord of the Treasury

Deep in the lower vaults of Singe's castle sits the Lizard King -- a hulking reptilian warlord draped in stolen finery. His golden crown sits atop a warty, scaled head. His yellow eyes gleam with avarice. He speaks with a long, hissing drawl, and he guards his treasure with a ferocity that rivals the dragon himself.

The Lizard King serves as Singe's lieutenant, charged with protecting the castle's stolen wealth. His chamber holds a massive enchanted pot of gold that has a sinister property: it magnetically pulls weapons from the hands of anyone who enters, leaving them defenseless before the Lizard King's fury.

### The Chase

When Dirk enters the treasury, the pot of gold rips his sword from his hands and flees down the winding corridors with it. What follows is a desperate chase -- Dirk sprinting after his stolen weapon while the Lizard King thunders behind him, swinging his golden scepter at every turn. At each intersection, you must choose the right path or be flattened.

One swing of that scepter can incapacitate a knight. The Lizard King is deceptively fast for his bulk, and he knows every twist and turn of his domain. He will not stop until the intruder is crushed.

### Seize the Moment

The only way to survive is to recover your sword from the pot of gold and turn it against the Lizard King. But even then, he will not go down easily -- expect to lose your weapon again before the fight is over.

**Strategy:** He is relentless, but vulnerable the instant after he swings. Wait for his attack, dodge it, and counter-strike! Watch out for the gold itself -- it can be slippery!
