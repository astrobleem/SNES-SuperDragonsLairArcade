**Bowling for Knights**

A long stone corridor stretches before you -- and at the far end, massive, brightly colored balls begin to roll toward Dirk with increasing speed. These are not toys. Each one is the size of a boulder, heavy enough to flatten a knight in full armor, and they bounce unpredictably off the walls and floor.

The corridor is narrow, leaving precious little room to dodge. Some balls are small enough to leap over; others fill the entire hallway and can only be avoided by pressing flat against the wall at the right moment.

*   **Threat:** Enormous balls rolling at high speed down the corridor. Getting hit means getting pancaked. Different sizes require different dodges.
*   **Action:** Dodge! Jump over the small ones. Press yourself flat against the wall for the massive ones. React to each ball individually -- no two come the same way.
*   **Tip:** Watch the color and size of each ball as it appears at the far end of the corridor. You have a split second to decide: jump or wall-press. Choose wrong and you are flat.
