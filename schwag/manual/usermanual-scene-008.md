**Ceiling Menace**

Something lives in the ceiling of this chamber -- something large, something hungry. Thick purple tentacles hang down from cracks in the stone above, swaying gently as if tasting the air. The moment Dirk enters, they come alive, lashing downward with terrifying speed to coil around anything they can reach.

At the center of the room sits an ornate treasure chest, practically begging to be opened. Is it bait? Almost certainly. But what choice does a knight have?

*   **Threat:** Giant purple tentacles strike from above, trying to wrap around you and drag you to the ceiling. The treasure chest in the center may be a lure.
*   **Action:** Slash the tentacles with your sword before they can coil around you! Cut them fast -- for every one you sever, another drops from the darkness above.
*   **Tip:** The tentacles have a tell: they sway wider just before they strike. Watch for the motion and swing your sword the moment they lunge.
