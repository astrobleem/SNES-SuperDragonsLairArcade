### Daughter of the King

Princess Daphne is the beloved daughter of King Ethelred and the fairest maiden in all the land. Many suitors sought her hand -- brave knights offered riches, handsome princes pledged their kingdoms -- but Daphne had already given her heart to the one man who deserved it: the clumsy, courageous, utterly devoted Dirk the Daring.

### Imprisoned in Crystal

When Singe the Dragon stormed the kingdom, it was Daphne he took as his prize. She was carried to the deepest chamber of the castle and sealed within an enchanted crystal sphere -- an unbreakable bubble of magical energy from which no mortal force can free her. There she waits, trapped but unbroken, watching through the shimmering walls of her prison for the knight she knows will come.

### More Than a Damsel

Do not mistake her captivity for helplessness. Daphne is sharp-witted and resourceful. When Dirk finally reaches the Dragon's Lair, it is Daphne herself who reveals the secret to defeating Singe: the golden key hung around the dragon's neck is the only thing that can shatter her prison, and the enchanted sword Dragonfang -- hidden somewhere in the dragon's hoard -- is the only weapon that can slay him.

Without her guidance, even the bravest knight would fall.

*The key to her release lies around the dragon's neck!*
