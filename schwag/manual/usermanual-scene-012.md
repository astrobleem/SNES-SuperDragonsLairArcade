# SCENE 12: ELEVATOR FLOOR

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-012.svg)

![Elevator Floor](images/flrp_fixed_shot.png)

**Going Up?**

The floor of this chamber is divided into sections that rise and fall independently, like the keys of some enormous, deranged organ. Some platforms shoot upward toward the crushing ceiling; others plunge downward into darkness. Phantom strings shimmer in the air, hinting at the invisible mechanisms that control this vertical maze.

Dirk must leap from platform to platform, timing each jump to catch a rising section before it drops away beneath him. One miscalculation means falling into the void below -- or being crushed against the ceiling above.

*   **Threat:** Platforms rise and fall unpredictably. You can be crushed against the ceiling or dropped into the pit below.
*   **Action:** Jump to the next platform at precisely the right moment! Watch the phantom strings for clues about which sections will move next.
*   **Tip:** The platforms follow a repeating cycle. Watch one full cycle before jumping in. The phantom strings glow brighter just before a platform moves.
