**A Deadly Trap**

At first glance, the Bower appears to be one of the few safe havens in the castle -- a quiet sitting room with ornate furnishings and decorative stonework. Do not be fooled. The moment Dirk steps inside, the room comes alive with malice. The ceiling begins to descend, the walls crack and buckle inward, and massive chunks of masonry crash to the floor.

Mordroc designed this room as a trap for overconfident adventurers -- a place that invites you to let your guard down, then crushes you for the mistake.

*   **Threat:** The ceiling and walls collapse inward. Massive stone blocks fall without warning. The entire room is designed to flatten you.
*   **Action:** React quickly to each falling hazard! Dodge left, right, and leap for safety as the room crumbles around you. There is no time to plan -- only react.
*   **Tip:** The Bower is one of the game's most deceptive scenes. It looks calm, then erupts into chaos. Watch for the visual cues and be ready to move in any direction.
