# THE LEGEND BEGINS

## Story

![The Adventure Begins](images/intr_fixed_shot.png)

*Lead on, Adventurer! Your quest awaits!*

Long ago, in a kingdom besieged by darkness, the evil wizard Mordroc unleashed his most terrible servant upon the land: **Singe**, a dragon of immense power and insatiable greed. Singe descended upon the kingdom of King Ethelred, burning villages, scattering armies, and claiming the king's own castle as his lair.

But Mordroc's cruelty did not end there. By the dragon's claws, **Princess Daphne** -- the king's beloved daughter and the fairest maiden in all the realm -- was snatched from the royal court and imprisoned deep within the castle. Sealed inside an unbreakable crystal sphere by Mordroc's dark magic, she awaits rescue in the deepest chamber of the Dragon's Lair, guarded by Singe himself.

King Ethelred was given an ultimatum: surrender his entire kingdom by sunset, or the Princess would perish.

Now, only one knight is brave enough -- or perhaps foolish enough -- to answer the call. You are **Dirk the Daring**, a knight of incredible courage but perhaps questionable coordination. Armed with nothing but your trusty sword and your wits, you must venture alone into Singe's castle, survive the wizard's deadly traps, defeat the monsters that lurk in every shadow, and reach the Dragon's Lair to save Princess Daphne.

The castle is vast. Its 29 chambers are filled with bottomless pits, living doors, enchanted weapons, and creatures that defy description. Every room is a death trap. Every corridor hides a new horror. And deep below it all, Singe sleeps atop his mountain of stolen gold, waiting.

## How to Play

This is not a platformer. This is not an RPG. This is an **interactive cartoon** -- a faithful recreation of the legendary 1983 arcade classic that revolutionized gaming, now running on your Super Nintendo with full-motion video powered by the MSU-1 chip.

You do not control Dirk's every step. Instead, you watch the beautifully animated scenes unfold and **react at the critical moment**. When danger strikes -- a floor collapses, a monster lunges, a blade swings -- you must press the correct direction or swing your sword with split-second timing.

**Watch closely. React fast. Timing is everything.**

## The Object of the Game

Guide Dirk through all 29 rooms of the castle. In each scene, you will face a life-or-death situation. Use your D-pad to dodge, duck, jump, and run. Use any face button to swing your sword. A brief **yellow flash** will cue you to act -- but the window is short. Miss it, and Dirk meets a creative and often spectacular end.

Only the bravest -- and those with the sharpest reflexes -- will reach the Dragon's Lair and save Princess Daphne. Good luck, Sir Knight. You are going to need it.
