# SCENE 15: FLAMING ROPES

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-015.svg)

![Flaming Ropes](images/flrp_fixed_shot.png)

**Hot Swing**

A bottomless pit stretches before you, spanned only by a series of dangling ropes. Simple enough -- until you grab the first one and it bursts into flame. Every rope in this room is enchanted to ignite the moment it is touched. The fire races up from the bottom, climbing toward your hands with alarming speed.

You have no choice but to swing. Grab a rope, build momentum, and leap to the next one before the flames reach you. Then do it again. And again. The pit below offers no second chances.

*   **Threat:** Each rope ignites on contact! The fire climbs rapidly toward your hands. The pit below is bottomless.
*   **Action:** Swing fast and jump to the next rope before the fire reaches you! Do not hesitate on any rope -- the flames wait for no one.
*   **Tip:** Rhythm is everything. Grab, swing, jump -- grab, swing, jump. If you pause to think, you burn. Trust your reflexes and keep the momentum going.
