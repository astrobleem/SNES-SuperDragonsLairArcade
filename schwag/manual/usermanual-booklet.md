# Super Dragon's Lair Arcade
## Official Player Manual (Booklet Draft)

Welcome to the castle, brave adventurer.
This draft booklet is a lightweight print-friendly version of the full in-repo manual.

## Story Setup
Princess Daphne has been captured, and Singe the Dragon guards the path ahead.
You play as Dirk the Daring, making split-second choices through dangerous rooms.

## Core Controls
- D-Pad: Move Dirk in the prompted direction.
- A / B / X / Y: Confirm directional timing prompts in scene events.
- Start: Pause.
- Select: Toggle HUD helpers (if enabled in build/config).

## Survival Tips
- Watch enemy tells before acting.
- Some rooms require memorization and rhythm.
- Missing one prompt often means instant danger, so stay focused.
- Practice Scene Select runs to learn difficult routes.

## Scene Highlights
- Drawbridge: Learn the timing language quickly.
- Tentacles & Halberd: Commit to your route and react fast.
- Underground River: Keep calm and chain clean movements.
- Dragon's Lair: The final gauntlet tests everything.
