# SCENE 17: BUBBLING CAULDRON

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-017.svg)

![Bubbling Cauldron](images/bcld_fixed_shot.png)

**Double Bubble, Toil and Trouble**

In a vast underground chamber, an enormous cauldron sits over an open flame, its contents bubbling with a sickly green luminescence. Toxic fumes rise in thick clouds, and the liquid inside churns and spits, sending globs of boiling slime arcing through the air. Something is stirring in the brew -- something that should not exist.

This is a wizard's cauldron, and whatever Mordroc has been cooking up in here, it is not soup. Acid creatures and strange astral beings emerge from the bubbling liquid, their forms shifting and dissolving as they reach for Dirk.

*   **Threat:** Boiling green slime erupts from the cauldron. Acid creatures emerge from the brew. Toxic fumes fill the air. One splash and you dissolve.
*   **Action:** Do NOT fall in! Navigate around the rim or use the platforms above. Avoid the splashing slime and the creatures that rise from the cauldron.
*   **Tip:** The cauldron erupts in rhythmic bursts. Watch the bubbling pattern and move during the calm moments between eruptions.
