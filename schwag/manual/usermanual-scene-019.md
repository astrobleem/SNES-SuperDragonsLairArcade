**The Dead Awaken**

Stone coffins line the walls of this ancient burial chamber, their lids cracked and askew. The air is cold and still -- deathly still. Then a pale, skeletal hand pushes a coffin lid aside, and the first ghoul crawls forth. Then another. And another. The crypt is waking up, and its residents are hungry.

Bouncing skulls clatter across the floor, flesh-eating insects swarm from the dark corners, and the ghouls advance with long, grasping arms. This is a place of the dead, and the dead do not appreciate visitors.

*   **Threat:** Ghouls emerge from coffins with long, grasping reach. Bouncing skulls and swarming insects add to the chaos. The undead keep coming.
*   **Action:** Send them back to the grave with your sword! Be aggressive -- do not give the ghouls time to surround you. Slash fast and push through.
*   **Tip:** The ghouls are slow but persistent. Focus on the ones directly in your path rather than trying to clear the entire room. Cut your way through and do not look back.
