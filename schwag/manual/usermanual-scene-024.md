# SCENE 24: THE GRIM REAPER

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-024.svg)

![Grim Reaper](images/gr_fixed_shot.png)

**Death's Door**

A narrow stone bridge stretches across a bottomless void. At its center floats a cloaked figure -- featureless beneath its pointed hood, a gleaming halberd gripped in skeletal hands. This is the Shapeshifter, the castle's most ancient and terrible guardian. Some call it the Grim Reaper. Some call it the Thorn Lord. All who have seen it call it Death.

Twin whirling batons rotate on either side of the bridge like spinning blades, and the Reaper's scythe sweeps in slow, deliberate arcs. One touch from any of these means the end. But even if you strike the Reaper down, the danger is not over -- enchanted thorn vines explode from every surface the moment the specter falls, growing at terrifying speed to fill the entire chamber.

*   **Threat:** The Grim Reaper's scythe delivers instant death on contact. Twin whirling batons guard the approach. After the Reaper falls, thorn vines rapidly fill the room.
*   **Action:** Time your approach through the spinning batons, then strike the Reaper with your sword! But do NOT celebrate -- the moment it falls, RUN. The thorns grow fast and will consume the entire hall.
*   **Tip:** The batons have a rhythm. Watch them spin and dash through during the gap. After defeating the Reaper, you have only seconds before the thorns block the exit. Sprint!
