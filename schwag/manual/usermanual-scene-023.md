# SCENE 23: THE SMITHEE (REVERSED)

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-023.svg)

![Smithee Reversed](images/smr_fixed_shot.png)

**Mirror Match**

You thought you were done with the cursed blacksmith? Think again. The castle's corridors have looped you back to the Smithee's forge -- but this time, everything is reversed. The room is a mirror image of your first encounter: left is right, right is left, and every reflex you built in the first visit will betray you here.

The Smithee awakens again, just as furious as before. The enchanted weapons fly from the opposite walls. Your muscle memory screams to dodge one way, but the danger comes from the other.

*   **Threat:** The same cursed blacksmith and flying weapons, but horizontally mirrored. Your learned reflexes from Scene 22 will work against you.
*   **Action:** Reverse your instincts! Dodge the opposite direction from what feels natural. The Smithee still falls to one clean hit -- you just have to reach him from the other side.
*   **Tip:** This is one of the game's most devious tricks: using your own experience against you. Stay calm, read the visual cues fresh, and ignore what you remember from the first encounter.
