# SCENE 3: THE MIST ROOM

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-003.svg)

![Mist Room](images/mudm_fixed_shot.png)

**The Mudmen's Lair**

A thick, unnatural fog blankets the floor of this subterranean chamber, hiding what lurks beneath. The air is heavy with the stench of swamp and decay. Then the mud begins to move. Shapeless forms rise from the muck -- the Mudmen, reaching with grasping hands to drag the unwary beneath the surface.

These are the subterranean caves below Singe's castle, where boiling lava meets stagnant swamp water. The Mudmen are animated sludge, and anyone they catch becomes one of them -- assimilated into the mire forever.

*   **Threat:** Mudmen rising from the swamp in waves, trying to grab and engulf you. They multiply by assimilating their victims.
*   **Action:** Use your sword to fend them off, but do not stand and fight! They are too numerous and too strong. Keep moving toward the door before you are overwhelmed.
*   **Tip:** The Mudmen cannot be truly defeated -- cut one down and another rises from the mud. Your only real option is escape. Move forward, always forward.
