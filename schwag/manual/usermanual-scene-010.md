# SCENE 10: THE GIDDY GOONS

![Theme](images/theme_usermanual-scene-010.svg)

![Giddy Goons](images/gg_fixed_shot.png)

**Small but Deadly**

You hear the cackling first -- a shrill, monkey-like screeching that echoes up the narrow spiral staircase. Then the first purple shape drops from above, dagger in hand. Then another. And another. The Giddy Goons have found you, and they are delighted.

These feral, purple-skinned creatures are ambush predators that attack from both above and below on the cramped stairwell. They carry daggers and clubs, and their strength is in their numbers. Individually, one swing of Dirk's sword can cleave a Goon in half -- but they never come alone.

*   **Threat:** Giddy Goons swarm from above and below with daggers and clubs. They attack in waves and try to surround you on the narrow staircase.
*   **Action:** Fight them off with your sword! Strike the moment they appear -- fast, aggressive, no hesitation. Do not let them surround you.
*   **Tip:** The narrow staircase works in your favor if you keep moving. Fight them in a line, not a circle. Push forward, sword swinging, and do not stop until the screeching fades.
