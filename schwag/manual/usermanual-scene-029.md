**The Legend**

Before the quest begins, the castle tells its own story. The Attract Mode is a cinematic preview of Dirk's adventure -- a montage of the horrors, traps, and monsters that await within Singe's domain. Watch as the drawbridge crumbles, the Giddy Goons swarm, the Reaper raises his scythe, and the dragon stirs in his golden lair.

In the original 1983 arcade, the Attract Mode played on a loop to lure curious players to the cabinet with its stunning animation. Here on SNES, it serves the same purpose -- a taste of the adventure to come, rendered in full MSU-1 video glory.

*   **Content:** A cinematic preview of Dirk's journey through the castle, showcasing key scenes and enemies.
*   **Tip:** Watch closely! The attract mode shows real gameplay footage. Study the scenes and you may spot the correct reactions to some of the game's deadliest traps before you ever face them.
*   **Secret:** The attract mode loops continuously on the title screen. If you are stuck on a particular scene, let it play -- you might see the solution animated right before your eyes.
